# Artificial Intelligence, then (1960) and now | Querying machine learning movie ratings data with SPARQL

## Summary (EN)
The author describes his exploration of geographical information systems (GIS) data and how to use SPARQL queries to extract such data from DBpedia. He then visualizes this data using QGIS Desktop, focusing on the birthplaces of American astronauts over time to illustrate changes in their geographic distribution.
