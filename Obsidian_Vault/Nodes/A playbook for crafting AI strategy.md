# A playbook for crafting AI strategy

## Summary (EN)
This report, produced by MIT Technology Review Insights and sponsored by Boomi, offers a playbook for crafting an AI strategy. Based on global surveys of C-suite and senior data executives across various industries, the report identifies key challenges in scaling AI from pilots to enterprise-wide deployment, including issues related to governance, security, privacy, and data quality. It highlights that while most companies are already using AI, few have scaled beyond pilot projects. The report provides insights into the current state of AI adoption and offers strategic recommendations for organizations looking to integrate AI across their business.
