# Work, workforce, workers: Reinvented in the age of generative AI

## Summary (EN)
Generative AI is transforming the nature of work. It's essential to focus on human-centered change efforts and prepare workers for a generative AI world, ensuring that work improves for everyone while addressing ethical and societal issues like job displacement and data privacy.
