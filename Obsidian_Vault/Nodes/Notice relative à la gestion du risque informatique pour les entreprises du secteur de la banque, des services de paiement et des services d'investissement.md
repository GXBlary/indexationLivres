# Notice relative à la gestion du risque informatique pour les entreprises du secteur de la banque, des services de paiement et des services d'investissement

## Summary (EN)
This document provides guidance on the risk management framework for IT in banks, payment services and investment services companies. It aligns with EU directives and aims to enhance the overall risk management system by integrating new provisions into existing internal control regimes.
