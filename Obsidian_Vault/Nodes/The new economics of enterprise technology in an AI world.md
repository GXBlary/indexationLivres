# The new economics of enterprise technology in an AI world

## Summary (EN)
This report explores the relationship between enterprise technology spending and labor productivity in an AI-driven era, highlighting gaps between tech capabilities and actual performance. It discusses the shift towards operating expenditures due to cloud adoption and gen AI, as well as the impact of cybersecurity and compliance costs.
